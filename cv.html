Summary

Molecular biologist from Colombia and PhD student in Veterinary Medical Sciences (Wildlife Health and Ecology) at the University of Calgary, supported by an NSERC Canada Graduate Scholarship. My doctoral project studies emerging pathogens in wild mammals from Costa Rica. My background spans molecular biology, bioinformatics, the microbiome, tick-borne pathogens and space biology, with experience in molecular diagnostics, field sampling of wild mammals, zebrafish research on bone under simulated microgravity, and university teaching. Native Spanish speaker with advanced English.

Research areas: Microgravity Biology, Microbiome, Tick-borne Pathogens, Bioinformatics, Metagenomics, RT-qPCR, Biodiversity, STEM Education, Phylogenetics & Evolution, Disease Ecology, Mammalogy, Animal Welfare, Genomics, Transcriptomics, Molecular Diagnostics, Space Biology.
